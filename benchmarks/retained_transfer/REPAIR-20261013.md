campaign or retroactively changes its acceptance criteria or outcomes.

## Confirmed findings

- Original acquisition: 166 proposals; training match counts are 0 (159) or
  8 (7). Nine proposals fail the rule-only guard. Lowering the 7/8 threshold
  would not increase yield. The post-hoc audit's 37 unclassifiable proposals
  were not an admission gate.
- Original retries often emit byte-identical wrong rules. They receive only a
  match count; previous proposals and specific failed applications are absent.
- E2B family L1-F07, 20 previously scored held-out inputs: automatic SDPA
  dispatch gives Text 20/20, Native 0/20 in BF16. Promoted FP32 operation
  arithmetic on the exactly represented BF16 weights still gives 20/20 and
  0/20. This is not an original FP32 checkpoint measurement.
- BF16 eager and BF16 math SDPA both give Text 20/20 and Native 20/20.
  Split/full last-suffix hidden states diverge substantially at layer zero
  under automatic SDPA, including in promoted FP32. This rules out a simple
  final-token BF16 rounding flip as the sole cause.
- Explicit FlashAttention-only dispatch has no available kernel on this
  model/profile. Its failed diagnostic is preserved. The exact faulty
  optimized backend has not yet been isolated; no upstream defect is claimed.

## Changes

`acquire.py --refine` supplies the previous rule and two failed training
applications to the next proposal, plus the failure count. Long prior-rule
feedback is explicitly an excerpt (512 characters); the canonical candidate
and all raw outputs are never truncated or rewritten. All feedback is drawn
from the training export. Three attempts and the 7/8 gate remain unchanged in
this development pilot. Existing default invocation semantics are retained.

`PrefixReceiver` uses math SDPA for Gemma4 by default, for both full-text and
split-native calls. Other model families retain automatic dispatch. Explicit
`sdpa_backend='auto'` is available for separately labeled diagnostics. This is
a correctness containment in the HF reference path, not a demonstrated repair
of the shipped Rust/vLLM adapter. Performance must be remeasured under the
corrected arithmetic backend.

## Subsequent proposal interpretation

An extended, evaluator-only audit interprets bracket placeholders and nested
JSON strings. Of 166 original proposals, 7 match the reference template, 143
have a different extractable template, and 16 remain unresolved prose. This
changes no scored output or admission decision. First proposals: 7 matching,
47 different and 6 unresolved. Three unresolved first proposals (L1-F13,
L1-F18, L2-F14) contain useful rules plus worked training examples and account
for all 60 additional correct learner outputs under Unvalidated (20 each).
The rule-only guard rejects them before application validation. Thus the
aggregate 200-versus-140 loss is accounted for by record-policy rejection in
this campaign, not by a 7-versus-6 match threshold.

A new opt-in `--record-policy experience-derived` permits worked training
examples while retaining the same application-validation gate. Its system and
user proposal instructions agree. This is an explicit new experimental record
policy, not a correction to original scores or silent reclassification of the
original study. The currently running refinement pilot remains rule-only;
its frozen remote source has not been modified.
